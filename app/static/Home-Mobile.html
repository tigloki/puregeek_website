About PureGeek

PureGeek is a one-person practice built on a simple idea: nobody needs to talk down to you or bury you in technical jargon. Even complex technical concepts can be explained to non-technical users in an understandable and respectful way. The fact that my hobby, and often my vocation, for the last several decades has been centered around computers and the internet does not mean I am part of some secret society that you don't have the secret handshake for.

Woodworking, gardening, swimming pool chemistry, baking, car mechanics, economics, foreign policy, astrophysics, and why my dog never farts unless he's sleeping right at my feet are all things I wish I knew more about. But I spent my time learning all this stuff instead.

I have said it to many customers who warn me that they aren't technical people:

"Don't worry, I'm geek enough for both of us!"
