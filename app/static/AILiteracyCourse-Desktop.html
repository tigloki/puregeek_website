AI Literacy Course

"AI is like a playground merry-go-round that is already spinning. Everyone on it is having fun! But at this point, how do I get on?"

We can help.

When AI hit the scene, did you find it:

  • Off-putting?
  • Icky?
  • Scary?
  • Too complex?
  • Too ... inscrutable?
  • All of the above?

Regardless, for whatever reason, it's been here a while now and you're hesitant to ask. When the AI Hype-storm hit, you, sensibly, shuttered the windows, bolted the door, stoked the fire, and settled in to wait for it to blow over. Except that it hasn't blown over, has it? And at this point you're... a Late Adopter.

And who would you ask anyway? Your nephew? Your co-worker? 800 AI Videos on YouTube? No. None of that. You're going to ask us. Why? We won't talk down to you. We won't start in the middle. You won't be an overnight expert. You will, however be able to tell an AI about that 3-ingredient recipe you saw on social media and get the scoop on the nutritional information and actual measurements to make it work right.
